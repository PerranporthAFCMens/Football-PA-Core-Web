Why V3.1 behaves this way

Learned from three completed St Piran seasons

Locked model rules: opponent strength is recursive; home/away has a modest effect; goal margin is capped; early ratings shrink towards 100; Cup/CC matches are excluded; P-P and A-A are ignored; walkovers may affect league points but not playing strength; close ratings are shown as clusters and finishing bands rather than fake precision.
